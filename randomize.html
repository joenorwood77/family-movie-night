    Shuffle

    The new rotation is never the same as last season's. Each person's two categories are dealt at random between the first and second half, and everyone's eight free picks are spread across the eight free rounds.

    Step 4

    Lock it in

    Locking saves this order for everyone. Only the shuffle number is stored, which is enough to rebuild the whole season exactly. Shuffling again before you lock costs nothing.

    Step 5

    Trade weeks

    Swap who is up on two nights. Joe did this in Season 4 so his two Kill Bill films could run back to back. The person and the film move; the dates do not.
